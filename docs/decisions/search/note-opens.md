# Search ranks by what people open

### Every open of a note is logged in its workspace's object, and search ranks a note that holds every word by its opens

Dev2, 2026-11-22: "count the number of times a note has actually been opened by the user and a separate count for in total, and weight that heavily in nomination and ranking"; AI reads are "a separate metric ... a lot of times AI is discarding the results that people open"; and "keep the same count ... and then extract different things about who's opening it ... through what platform, through what AI client, through text, the user, was it a human".

- **One log, many signals.** Each open is a row in the workspace object's `ws_open_log` (`apps/mcp/src/workspaceStore/opens.js`): the note's row id (so a rename keeps its history), when, who, `by` (`person` or `ai`), the surface (`app`, `mcp`, or `text` for Tex) and the client's id and registered name. Counts are queries over the log, so a new signal never starts again at zero.
- **What is an open.** A person opening a note in the app (`POST /files/read`, `live/noteReadRoute.js`) is a person's open. A `read_note` or ChatGPT `fetch` that handed the note back is an AI read, logged with the person it was for (`tools/dispatch.js`, `search/opens.js` `countAiRead`). Being listed in a search answer is never an open.
- **How it ranks.** The search step "Opened before" (`opens`, after "Lower logs and archive") asks each workspace's object for the counts of the notes it already found and may show, and multiplies a row's score by `1 + openMine·log2(1 + your opens) + openPeople·log2(1 + everyone else's) + openAi·log2(1 + AI reads)`. "Rank across workspaces" applies the same multiplier. Main's weights are 0.3, 0.1 and 0.02.
- **Only a note the query is about rises.** The multiplier applies to a word hit that holds every word, never to a loose hit or a note found only by meaning. With it on every row, a note opened five times (a meeting page, a home page) jumped to first on unrelated questions and search-hard fell from 35/41 to 22/41. AI reads weigh least: at 0.05, twenty-five Tex reads of a runbook put it above the on-call rota the question was about.
- **Nomination is not changed yet.** An opened note that neither the word index nor meaning found is still not added. Pulling opened notes into the candidates is the next step, and it needs its own bench cases.
- **Privacy.** A search gets back only the asker's own count and totals, for paths it already found and may see, and never who else opened what. The log lives in the workspace's own object.

**Bench.** search-hard carries an open history (`packages/bench/benchmarks/search-hard/opens.json`), laid into its objects by `setUpWorkspace` locally and on the standing bench objects. It includes one `opened` case (`packing list`: Priya's own list against a wedding one) and decoys a bad weighting would lift (a home page opened 40 times, a runbook Tex read 25 times). Weights 0.3/0.1/0.02: search-hard 35 → 36 of 41, search-real unchanged.

**What a simplification would cost:** separate counters per signal, which lose every signal not thought of on day one; opens lifting loose or meaning-only rows (the home page outranks the answer); AI reads counted as people's (Tex reading a note makes it look chosen). **Tests:** `apps/mcp/test/searchOpens.test.mjs` (each sabotage-checked), and search-hard's `opened` case and decoys.
