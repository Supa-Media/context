# Search asks the index for what a question is about

### The words that only say how a question is asked are left out of the word index's query

Production, 2026-11-23, through the MCP: "what did we decide about bring your own bucket" and "what number do people text tex at now" returned meeting transcripts and archived chats, and the notes that answer them did not appear. The word index's first query needs every word of the question in one chunk. A note that states an answer rarely holds "what", "did" and "we", and a long transcript holds them all, so only transcripts matched, and the any-word retry, which would have found the answer, runs only when nothing matched.

- **What is left out.** `FILLER_WORDS` (`apps/mcp/src/search/text.js`, the three-letter-or-longer words already left out of title evidence) and the one- and two-letter words of a question (`a`, `at`, `do`, `we`, ...: `SHORT_FILLER`, `apps/mcp/src/search/d1/query.js`).
- **Only where every word already matched.** `toContentMatchExpression` rides in the same request as the strict expression, and `answerFromProjection` (`d1/serve.js`) ranks its rows only when the strict expression found some; they are a superset of them. Where nothing held every word, the any-word retry over every word stands, as before. The first version left filler out of every expression, and the gate on real embeddings refused it: the search Test part 4 of 10 against main's 5, NanoBEIR nDCG@10 0.516 against 0.545 (nq 0.512 → 0.445), because a natural question that no chunk answered word for word then required every content word instead of ranking by any of them.

**Bench.** search-hard's `asked` kind: three questions asked in a person's words, each beside a meeting transcript that quotes it (`what is the price for the caterer`, `where do we keep the spare key`, `who books the room for the thursday session`). Embedder faked: search-hard 37 → 37 of 44 (an asked question from a miss to first, another from a miss to third; one paraphrase 1 → 2), NanoBEIR gate 0.506 → 0.511 (the first version 0.491).

**What a simplification would cost:** asking every word again makes a question's "what did we" a requirement, and the notes that hold it are transcripts; leaving filler out everywhere loses the any-word retry natural questions rank best by. **Tests:** `apps/mcp/test/searchD1.test.mjs` and `test/searchProjection/oneTrip.test.mjs` (the asked-question block, sabotage-checked: it fails with the content rows unread), and search-hard's `asked` cases.

**A setting, so a draft can weigh it (2026-11-23):** the content-word match is the "Words" step's `contentWords` (1 on main, 0 off) in `searchConfig.json`. A Lab draft turns it off and benches it with `tex_dev search_bench` in seconds ([draft-bench-runs](./draft-bench-runs.md)) instead of a pull request per try. **Test:** `test/searchProjection/oneTrip.test.mjs` "with contentWords off, only the note holding every word is a hit" (sabotage-checked).
