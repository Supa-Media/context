# Band leader reading list

Books for Priya's year of leading the band, in the order to read them.

- *The Rehearsal Room* (running a practice without shouting)
- *Listen First* (short, about setlist arguments)
- *Four Chords and a Calendar* (booking gigs and keeping the money straight)
- *The Quiet Drummer* (giving feedback to people older than you)

Read the first two before the October session. The calendar book can wait until the gear fund is sorted.
