# Genesis 19

Previous: [[Genesis 18]]
Next: [[Genesis 20]]

## Overview

Two angels come to Sodom in the evening and Lot takes them into his house. The men of the city surround it and demand the guests be brought out to them. Lot offers his daughters instead; the angels strike the crowd blind and lead Lot's family out before the city is destroyed.

## Initial Read Through

The crowd at the door is the scene everyone remembers. Lot protecting guests at any cost, even his own daughters, is hard to read.
