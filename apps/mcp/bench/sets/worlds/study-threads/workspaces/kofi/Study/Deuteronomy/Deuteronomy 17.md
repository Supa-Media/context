# Deuteronomy 17

Previous: [[Deuteronomy 16]]
Next: [[Deuteronomy 18]]

## Overview

Laws on sacrifice, on judging hard cases at the central sanctuary, and, from verse 14, the law of the king: when Israel asks for a king like the nations around it, he must be one God chooses, must not multiply horses, wives or silver, and must write out a copy of the law and read it all his life.

## Initial Read Through

Interesting that kingship is expected here, but fenced in. The king is under the law, not above it.
