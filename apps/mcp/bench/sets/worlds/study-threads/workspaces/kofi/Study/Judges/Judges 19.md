# Judges 19

Previous: [[Judges 18]]
Next: [[Judges 20]]

## Overview

A Levite and his concubine travel home and stop for the night in Gibeah, a town of Benjamin. Men of the town surround the house; the concubine is pushed out to them and dies. The Levite cuts her body into twelve pieces and sends them through all Israel, which gathers for war against Benjamin.

## Initial Read Through

Gibeah keeps coming up as the bad town. The whole story makes Benjamin look as low as possible. Is the writer making a point about someone from Benjamin later on? Come back to this.
