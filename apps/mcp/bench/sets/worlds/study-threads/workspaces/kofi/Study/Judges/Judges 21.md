# Judges 21

Previous: [[Judges 20]]
Next: none

## Overview

After the war, Israel has sworn not to give its daughters to Benjamin. To keep the tribe alive they strike Jabesh-gilead, which sent no one to the assembly, and give its young women to the Benjaminites. The book ends: in those days there was no king in Israel; everyone did what was right in his own eyes.

## Initial Read Through

The last line sounds like an ad for a king. Jabesh-gilead gets wiped out for not showing up, which feels like a detail someone wanted remembered.
