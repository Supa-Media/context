# First EP mix log

A running log of the mixes for the first EP, one entry per round, newest at the bottom. Each entry says what changed and who asked for it. The masters go to the mastering house once the last round is signed off by the band.

## Round 1, rough balance

First pass from the rehearsal recording. Drums and vocal were level, the guitars were too wide, and the bass was lost under the kick. No effects yet; the aim was to hear the song.

- Pan the rhythm guitars 70 and 30.
- Bring the bass up until it sits under the kick, not on it.
- Vocal reverb is too long for the verse.

## Round 2, the drums

Theo asked for the snare to sit further forward. The overheads were thin, so the room mic came up a little. The kick now has more click and less boom.

- Snare forward by one dB at 180 hertz.
- Room mic up, kept low in the mix.
- Hi-hat is still too loud in the bridge; automate it down.

## Round 3, the vocal

Ivo and Sol listened on headphones and on the car stereo. The vocal was clear on headphones but buried in the car. The fix was a smaller de-esser and a touch more presence in the upper mids.

- De-esser threshold lifted, so the ess sounds stop jumping out.
- Presence boost at 3 kHz, half the amount from round 2.
- Doubled chorus vocal kept only for the last chorus.

## Round 4, the chorus

This was the round that took the longest. The chorus felt right on the monitors and wrong everywhere else, and it took three listens with the band in the room to find out why. The chorus has the bass, the two guitars, the keys and the vocal all in the same region, so the bottom end piles up.

Sol's note after the listen, word for word: "the bass is too loud in the chorus, drop it two dB, it fights the kick and the keys on the first beat". The band agreed. The bass is now down two dB in the chorus only, with a low shelf on the keys to keep the warmth. The verse bass stays where it was in round 3.

- Bass down 2 dB in the chorus only, as Sol asked.
- Low shelf on the keys to keep the warmth without the boom.
- Guitar two panned back toward centre for the last chorus.

## Round 5, the bridge

The bridge is the most exposed part of the song. The clean guitar came forward and the reverb tail was shortened to stop the wash over the next line. The count-in was redone with a click on the two and four, because the first count-in drifted.

- Clean guitar up 1.5 dB in the bridge.
- Reverb tail shortened to 1.2 seconds.
- Count-in is now a click, not a snare.

## Round 6, the ending

The last bar was cut short because the band wanted the final chord to ring out. The fade is now four bars, and the vocal ad-lib from round 3 sits under it, quiet.

## Round 7, the sign-off

Everyone listened to the full EP on the same device in the same room. Nothing was changed. The loudness target is minus fourteen LUFS for the streaming masters, and the mastering house has the stems and the notes.

## Notes for the next EP

Record the rehearsal on two devices next time, so the reference is not the only copy. Check every mix in the car, not only on headphones. Keep the bass decisions in the log, because the bass was the hardest part of this EP and the reasons are easy to forget.

## Round 8, the intro

The intro was cut from forty seconds to twenty-two. The synth pad that opened the song was too wide for a streaming listener on a phone, so it was mono below 200 hertz. The count-in from round five is used as the first sound, which everyone agreed was better than the old intro.

- Intro cut to 22 seconds.
- Pad mono below 200 hertz.
- Count-in moved to the first bar.

## Round 9, the second verse

Theo said the second verse felt thinner than the first. The guitar part had changed in the rehearsal and the mix had not caught up. The fix was to bring the rhythm guitar two back up and to let the keys drop an octave in the top line, so the voice has room.

- Rhythm guitar two up by 1 dB in verse two.
- Keys top line dropped an octave for verse two only.
- Shaker added on the off-beat, very quiet, for the groove.

## Round 10, the last check

A final pass on the whole EP at the same level, with the band on the same device. Ivo noticed that the snare in the first song clipped on the phone speaker. The limiter was backed off by 0.5 dB on the drums bus, and the rest of the EP was not touched.

## Mastering notes

The mastering house asked for stems with the bass and the kick in separate files, and a note of every change after round seven. The stems are in the band folder under the release name. The loudness target stays at minus fourteen LUFS for streaming, with a true peak of minus one. The house will send a reference master before the final, and the band has agreed to listen to that reference in the car as well as on headphones.

## Equipment used

The mixes were done on the monitors in the back room of the studio, with the headphones for checking only. The compressor on the vocal is the old one from the gear fund, and the reverb is the plug-in the band already owns. No new gear was bought for the EP, and the gear fund is the same as it was before the recordings started.

## Round 11, the outro

The outro was the last thing changed. The keys were brought down for the final two bars, and the vocal ad-lib from round three was moved so it lands on the last beat instead of before it. Ivo asked for the reverb on the ad-lib to end before the fade starts, and it does now, by about a quarter of a second.

## Timings

The final running time is three minutes and forty seconds for the full EP, with the shortest song at two minutes and six seconds and the longest at four minutes and eleven. The first song is the one with the bass change, which the band now calls the Sol song when they talk about the mix.

## Who listened

The band listened together at the studio, in the car, on the phone speaker and on the stereo at Sol's place. Theo listened only on the monitors and was overruled twice. The final sign-off was given in a group call after everyone had heard the masters on their own device.
