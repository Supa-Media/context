# brunch.example

An invented brunch guide, for the everyday tasks (`tests/everyday.md` and `tests/everyday-groups.md`). From the
reviewed chats: asked for brunch spots with rough prices, Tex stopped after one result, gave prices and hours it
never checked, or listed a place that was closed. The list page names four places with no hours or prices; each
place's own page has its Sunday hours and its price range, and one is closed on Sundays, so a good answer opens all
four.

## https://brunch.example/
title: Riverside Brunch Guide
text: Where to brunch in Riverside. Tap a place for its hours and prices.
