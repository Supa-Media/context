# halls.example

An invented event-space directory, for the hand-back bench
(`tests/handback.md` case 17). From a guest group chat's self-review of
2026-10-27 (9d98ef): asked for five Manhattan venues for Sunday services,
Tex listed two that churches already meet in on Sundays, dropped to three and
offered "Want me to find two more?", and set the directory's general price
range beside a venue that publishes none. Here the list page names six halls
with no prices or Sunday use; each hall's own page says whether a church
already meets there on Sundays and what it charges. Two are church homes on
Sundays, so only four fit, and Ferry House publishes no price.
