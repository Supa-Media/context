# Walnut credenza (home version)

A credenza for the living room, sized to the alcove.

- Final width: 1,500 mm. Height 760 mm.
- Walnut boards are drying in the stack. Check moisture before cutting.
- Joinery: floating tenons on the carcass, dovetails on the drawers.
- The full build log is in the woodshop workspace under projects/walnut-credenza.md.
