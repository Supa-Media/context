# Woodshop

John's woodshop, where he builds furniture: projects, tools, lumber and shop rules. John is the only member.

- `projects/` — the walnut credenza, the porch bench and the commission for Ruth.
- `tools/` — tool inventory and the sharpening schedule.
- `suppliers/` — the lumber yard and hardware orders.
- `safety/` — shop rules.
- `inbox/` — unsorted photos and notes.
- `todo.md` — open to-dos.
