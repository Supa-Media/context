---
job: search
search:
  everywhere: true
came_from: words-and-meaning, with one search across every workspace
why: An assistant starts in the person's own workspace and has to guess where an answer lives; the rounds showed the cheap model guessing wrong or stopping after one place. With everywhere on, one call searches every workspace the person can reach, each under their role there and its own privacy rules, and fuses one list with the workspace's name in front of a path from elsewhere.
---

The defaults, and `search_notes` with no `context` searches every workspace the person can reach.
